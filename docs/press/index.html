Terra Live Wallpapers: press kit

One line: The real Earth as a live wallpaper: real satellite clouds, true sunlight, aurora, city lights, the Moon at true scale. Nothing is a loop; everything corresponds to something actually happening.

Developer: solo founder, contact via the Play listing.

Facts that make the story

Built by one person for about 25 dollars total. The wallpaper computes real astronomy from the clock (GMST rotation, solar ephemeris, lunar phase) and pulls live data: EUMETSAT and NOAA cloud composites, NOAA aurora forecasts, NASA fire and event feeds, daily NASA snow cover, AMSR2 sea ice, live ISS position. Weather-model data covers the poles that geostationary satellites cannot see. On capable phones the planet renders from 8K NASA imagery.
