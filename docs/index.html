Questions

Is it a video? No. The planet is computed live on your phone. The clouds you see were photographed by weather satellites hours ago at most, and the sun sets on the wallpaper when it sets outside your window.
Battery? Rendering pauses completely when hidden and the frame rate adapts when visible. Live wallpapers earned a bad name a decade ago; hardware and this engine both moved on.
Why is it paid? One small one-time price, no ads, no accounts, no tracking, no subscriptions. You buy an instrument, not a feed of ads.
What about the poles? Geostationary satellites physically cannot see them, so Terra splices in polar orbiter and weather model data. Honest data, honestly labeled.
