TERRA

The real Earth, live on your phone. Not a video. Not a loop. The actual planet, right now.
Get it on Google Play Watch the live demo
Live satellite clouds. Real weather from geostationary satellites, refreshed through the day, with a weather-model splice covering the poles that only polar orbiters see.
True sunlight. The day and night line is computed from the clock. Sunrise on your wallpaper is your sunrise.
A living night side. Real city lights, moonlit cloud decks, aurora painted from the live NOAA forecast, wildfires from NASA event feeds.
Honest astronomy. The Moon at true scale and true phase. Planets and 100,000 stars at their real positions. The ISS passing overhead, live.
Seasons for real. Daily snow cover and sea ice from NASA and AMSR2. Winter arrives on your phone when it arrives on Earth.
Quiet on your battery. Adaptive frame rate, full pause when hidden, one-time purchase, no ads, no accounts, no tracking.
